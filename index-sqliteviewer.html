SQLite Viewer is installed 🎉

One last step — pin it to your toolbar so it's always one click away.

1
Click the puzzle icon 🧩 in your browser's toolbar, top right.
2
Find SQLite Viewer in the list.
3
Click the pin icon next to it. It'll now stay visible in your toolbar.
4
Click the SQLite Viewer icon to open the side panel. Unlike a regular popup, it stays open while you browse other tabs.
5
Drag a .db / .sqlite file into the panel, or click the + button, to open it. If Chrome asks for permission to write to the file, allow it — that's what lets your edits save automatically.

💡 Everything runs locally in your browser. Your database is never uploaded anywhere.
