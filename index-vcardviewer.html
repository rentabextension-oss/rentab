You're all set

Thanks for installing vCard Viewer. Here is how to pin it and open your first contact file.

One last step — pin it to your toolbar

1

Open the extensions menu

Click the puzzle-piece icon in the top-right corner of Chrome.

2

Pin vCard Viewer

Find vCard Viewer in the list and click the pin icon next to it, so its icon stays in your toolbar.

3

Open the side panel

Click the vCard Viewer icon in the toolbar. The viewer opens in the side panel, next to your current tab.

Open your first file

1

Drop a contact file in

Drag a .vcf or .vcard file into the panel, or press Choose file to pick one.

2

Read every contact as a card

Each contact becomes a clean card with name, photo, phones, emails, addresses and social profiles. Files with many contacts open as a list — click any name to open that card.

3

Edit, export or copy

Press the pencil to fix a contact and Save .vcf, export everything to CSV, or copy a single contact to the clipboard.

What it does

Open .vcf and .vcard

Reads contact files exported from Android, iPhone, Outlook, Gmail and macOS.

Edit and save

Fix a name, phone or address and write a clean .vcf file back to disk.

Export to CSV

Move contacts into Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets in one click.

Fully private

Files are read on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, no account, works offline.

Your contacts never leave your computer. vCard Viewer parses each file locally in the browser and keeps nothing after you close the panel.
