RenTab is installed 🎉

One last step — pin it to your toolbar so it's always one click away.

1
Click the puzzle icon 🧩 in your browser's toolbar, top right.
2
Find RenTab – rename tab in the list.
3
Click the pin icon next to it. It'll now stay visible in your toolbar.
4
Open the RenTab icon on any tab, type a custom title, and hit Save.
5
Need to find an older title or change how RenTab works? Open the History tab to search and revisit anything you've renamed, or the Settings tab to turn on Chrome Sync (so your titles follow you to other computers) or to export/import a backup of all your titles.
