Birthday Calendar is installed 🎉

One last step — pin it to your toolbar so it's always one click away.

1
Click the puzzle icon 🧩 in your browser's toolbar, top right.
2
Find Birthday Calendar — Reminders & Notifications in the list.
3
Click the pin icon next to it. It'll now stay visible in your toolbar.
4
Click the Birthday Calendar icon to open the side panel, then hit + Add to save your first birthday — name, date, and an optional birth year.
5
Want to change how reminders work? Open the Settings tab to choose which notifications you receive, turn on sync so your birthdays follow you to other devices, or export/import a backup of everything you've saved.
